# Data sources for data/khao_yai_500m.npz

Only derived 500 m cell aggregates are stored in this repository, not the source data. All sources were accessed on 12 November 2026 without accounts or API keys. `fetch_khao_yai.py` regenerates the extract; the main pipeline reads only the npz.

## Elevation: Copernicus DEM GLO-30

Tile N14_00_E101_00 (1 arc-second, about 30 m), read as a window over HTTP. Stored: mean and maximum pixel elevation per 500 m cell (`dem_m`, `dem_max_m`).
Source: https://copernicus-dem-30m.s3.eu-central-1.amazonaws.com/Copernicus_DSM_COG_10_N14_00_E101_00_DEM/Copernicus_DSM_COG_10_N14_00_E101_00_DEM.tif

## Elevation for the 3D view: Copernicus DEM GLO-30, native resolution

Same tile and source as the elevation entry above, read at native 1 arc-second (about 30 m) for the dashboard's 10 km x 10 km window only, with no resampling. Stored: `dashboards/public/terrain.bin` (unsigned 16-bit little-endian elevation in decimetres, 327 rows x 335 columns, row 0 = north, column 0 = west) and `terrain.json` (shape, range, edges). Used only for the dashboard's 3D terrain mesh; the analysis reads the 500 m aggregates. Generated by `export_terrain_mesh.py` on 12 November 2026.
Source: https://copernicus-dem-30m.s3.eu-central-1.amazonaws.com/Copernicus_DSM_COG_10_N14_00_E101_00_DEM/Copernicus_DSM_COG_10_N14_00_E101_00_DEM.tif
Licence: Copernicus DEM licence (free, including commercial use, with attribution).
Attribution: produced using Copernicus WorldDEM-30 © DLR e.V. 2010-2014 and © Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014-2018 provided under COPERNICUS by the European Union and ESA; all rights reserved

## Fire detections: NASA FIRMS

VIIRS S-NPP 375 m active fire detections, yearly Thailand CSVs for 2012 to 2025. Kept: confidence `n` or `h` and `type` == 0 (presumed vegetation fire). Stored: detection count per cell (`fire_count`) and number of distinct years with a detection (`fire_years`).
Source: https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/data/country/viirs-snpp/YEAR/viirs-snpp_YEAR_Thailand.csv
Licence: NASA open data policy; free to use with acknowledgement.
Attribution: We acknowledge the use of data and/or imagery from NASA's Fire Information for Resource Management System (FIRMS) (https://earthdata.nasa.gov/firms), part of NASA's Earth Science Data and Information System (ESDIS).

## Land cover: ESA WorldCover 2021 v200

10 m land-cover map, tile N12E099, read as a window over HTTP. Stored: share of pixels per class in each cell (tree, shrub, grass, crop, built, bare, water, wetland, other).
Source: https://esa-worldcover.s3.eu-central-1.amazonaws.com/v200/2021/map/ESA_WorldCover_10m_2021_v200_N12E099_Map.tif
Licence: CC BY 4.0.
Attribution: © ESA WorldCover project 2021 / Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data (2021) processed by ESA WorldCover consortium
Citation: Zanaga et al. 2022, doi 10.5281/zenodo.7254221

Derived file for the 3D view: `dashboards/public/landcover.bin` and `landcover.json`, generated by `data/export_landcover_mesh.py` on 12 November 2026 from the same tile, read as a window over HTTP. On the pixel grid of `terrain.bin` (327 x 335, row 0 north, column 0 west) it stores per pixel the majority class of the 10 m pixels inside it and the share of tree-cover pixels (0 to 255). Same source, licence and attribution as above.

## Fire history for date replay: NASA FIRMS

Same VIIRS S-NPP 375 m yearly Thailand CSVs as above, 2012 to 2025, filtered to the dashboard's map window with the same row filter (confidence `n` or `h`, type == 0). Stored: `dashboards/public/fires.json`, one row per detection (date, lon, lat, FRP, grid row and column, night flag). Source, licence and attribution as in the fire detections entry above.

## Hazard-training grid: `hazard_training_500m.npz`

`data/fetch_hazard_training.py` builds a 200 x 200 grid of 500 m cells (about 100 km x 100 km) centred on, and on the same lattice as, the Khao Yai extract above (the extract is the block at rows 80 to 119, columns 80 to 119). It reuses the same elevation (Copernicus DEM GLO-30, here four tiles N13/N14 x E100/E101), land-cover (ESA WorldCover 2021), park-boundary and FIRMS (2012 to 2025, same filters) sources with the same licences and attribution as the entries above; the FIRMS counts are stored per year. Details, download dates and check results are in `hazard_training_meta.json`.

## Roads and settlements: OpenStreetMap via Overpass

Highways of class motorway, trunk, primary, secondary, tertiary, unclassified, residential and track (way geometry) and place nodes of type city, town, village and hamlet, in a bounding box 10 km larger than the grid on every side. Stored: distance from each cell centre to the nearest road (`dist_road_m`), nearest motorway-to-tertiary road (`dist_major_road_m`) and nearest settlement (`dist_settlement_m`); no OSM geometry is stored. One Overpass request per feature class; raw responses are cached outside the repository.
Source: Overpass API, https://overpass-api.de/api/interpreter (fallback mirrors in the script: overpass.kumi.systems, overpass.private.coffee); the endpoint that served each class is recorded in `hazard_training_meta.json`.
Licence: ODbL 1.0, https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
Attribution: © OpenStreetMap contributors

## Fire-season wind: Open-Meteo historical archive

Hourly 10 m wind speed and direction for 1 February to 30 April, 2012 to 2024 (27,864 hours), at one point: the centre of the 40 x 40 grid (lat 14.389204, lon 101.200872). Stored: `data/wind_fire_season.npz` (`time_utc`, `speed_ms`, `direction_deg` meteorological, the direction the wind blows from, and the grid point used). Generated by `data/fetch_wind.py` on 13 November 2026.
Source: https://archive-api.open-meteo.com/v1/archive (one request per year, `hourly=wind_speed_10m,wind_direction_10m`, `wind_speed_unit=ms`, `timezone=UTC`)
Point used: the API reports lat 14.446397, lon 101.26043 (elevation 685 m). Open-Meteo picks a land grid cell near the requested coordinate, so the value is a few kilometres from the centre.
Underlying model: the default "Best Match" blends ERA5 and ERA5-Land reanalysis (Copernicus C3S). Those grids are about 0.1 to 0.25 degrees, roughly 10 to 25 km. The grid is much coarser than the 20 km map window, so this is one wind value per hour for the whole map, not a per-cell field. Open-Meteo did not report which component supplied each hour.
Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), https://open-meteo.com/en/licence
Attribution: Weather data by Open-Meteo.com (https://open-meteo.com). Reanalysis: weather reanalysis from the Copernicus Climate Change Service (C3S), as listed on the Open-Meteo licence page; the Copernicus licence document is the reference for exact ERA5 wording (not fetched).

## Whole-park grid: `park_500m.npz`

`data/fetch_park_grid.py` builds a 131 x 187 grid of 500 m cells covering the entire park (bounding box of the OpenStreetMap park polygon plus 10 cells, 5 km, on every side) on the same lattice as the 200 x 200 and 40 x 40 grids above: cell (r, c) is cell (r + 48, c + 73) of the 200 x 200 grid, and the 40 x 40 extract is the block at rows 32 to 71, columns 7 to 46. The 200 x 200 grid stops at longitude 101.664, so it misses the park's eastern part; this one does not. Stored per cell: elevation (mean and max), land cover, park share, distance to the park edge, FIRMS detections per year (2012 to 2025) and their sum. Same sources, filters, licences and attribution as the entries above (Copernicus DEM GLO-30 tile N14 E101, ESA WorldCover 2021 tile N12 E099, NASA FIRMS VIIRS S-NPP, OpenStreetMap park boundary). Not included: road and settlement distances; they are only needed to refit the hazard model, and the cached OpenStreetMap extract stops short of the park's east edge. Details, tile bounds and check results are in `park_500m_meta.json`.

## Whole-park dashboard files: `dashboards/public/park_terrain.*`, `park_landcover.*`, `park.json`

`data/export_park_dashboard.py` (run 13 November 2026) resamples elevation and land cover to a 437 x 623 grid of about 150 m pixels over the bounds of the whole-park grid above. Elevation is the mean of the Copernicus DEM GLO-30 pixels in each output pixel (tile N14 E101); land cover is the majority ESA WorldCover 2021 v200 class and the tree-cover share of the 10 m pixels in each output pixel (tile N12 E099). Both are read as windows from the same public files as the entries above; nothing is downloaded whole. `park.json` holds layers from `park_500m.npz` (real elevation, fire detections and park share), the modelled priority and visibility, and the placements and simulated detection times from `park/results.json`. Sources, licences and attribution as in the elevation, land cover, fire detections and park boundary entries above.
